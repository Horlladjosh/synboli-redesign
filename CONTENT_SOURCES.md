# Content basis for the redesign

Reviewed against Synboli's public website on 12 October 2026.

- Home and About Synboli: https://www.synboli.com/synboli
- Applications overview: https://www.synboli.com/solutions
- Industry detail: https://www.synboli.com/solutions/{cosmetics,pharma,transportation,electronics,construction,defense}
- Technology and Services: shared process and service sections on the industry pages.
- Jobs: https://www.synboli.com/jobs
- Contact: https://www.synboli.com/contact

The brief is a rich redesign that preserves the company's key messages. Copy can expand and explain these messages, without adding unverified offerings, results, customers or scientific claims.

Retained messages: AI plus patented chemistry; access to new polymer structures; computational and laboratory evaluation; development from years toward months; shorter time to market; tailored material properties; competitive advantage; six industry areas; original technical examples and named polymer families; four stages from application-specific data through scale-up; three service routes; two stated service timeframes; research origins and support; recruitment and contact details.

The redesign adds explanatory transitions and visual sections. Shared Technology and Services content has dedicated pages with summaries on industry pages. Stock images are illustrative, with separate credits. The Contact form prepares an email draft rather than submitting to the original embedded form.
